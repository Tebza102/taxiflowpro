# Business Case

## Business Problem
Describe the problem from the buyer or user perspective.

## Why It Matters
Explain the cost of not solving the problem.

## Target Outcome
Describe the measurable business result expected.

## Value Proposition
Write the simple promise: this product helps [user] achieve [result] by [method].

## Commercial Model
Choose one: once-off sale, subscription, internal productivity tool, lead generator, agency service, freemium, enterprise license, support retainer.

## Revenue Logic
Explain how the project can make or save money.

## Risks to Commercial Success
List market, pricing, adoption, trust, implementation, and support risks.

## Minimum Proof Needed
Define the smallest evidence that proves the idea deserves more investment.
