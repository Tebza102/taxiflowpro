# Video Editing Skill

## Purpose
Use this skill to plan and direct edits for reels, shorts, long-form videos, business profiles, event edits, product videos, training videos, and website hero loops.

## Quality Standard
Use award-level quality thinking as an internal benchmark, not a guarantee.

## Anti-Generic Editing Rules
Avoid:
- flashy transition spam
- random zooms
- unreadable captions
- cluttered overlays
- pacing that ignores platform context

Prioritize story, clarity, and business credibility.

## 3D Context Rule
If edits include generated 3D scenes or 3D reveal sequences, align look and constraints with `3d-design-skill.md` direction to keep materials, lighting, camera logic, and brand stability consistent.

## Website-Bound Video Rule
If the output video will become a website scroll/canvas sequence, optimize for clean frame continuity, stable subject placement, minimal disruptive cuts, and scroll-friendly pacing, then hand off to `video-to-website-skill.md`.

## Required Output
1. Editing diagnosis
2. Structure and hook strategy
3. Scene/cut sequence
4. Caption/text overlay plan
5. Music/sound direction
6. Transition rules
7. Color grade direction
8. Export settings
9. Editing prompt for the chosen tool
10. Quality checklist
