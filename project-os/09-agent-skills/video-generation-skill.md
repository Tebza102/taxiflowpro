# Video Generation Skill

## Purpose
Use this skill to produce brief-led AI video prompts and scene direction for text-to-video, image-to-video, brand videos, product reveals, social videos, and website hero motion.

## Quality Standard
Use award-level quality thinking as an internal benchmark, not a guarantee.

## Anti-Generic Rules
Avoid:
- random flashy effects
- generic stock-video look
- meaningless 3D objects
- unstable logos/text/faces/products
- unreadable overlays

## 3D Integration Rule
When the task needs 3D scene direction, use `3d-design-skill.md` before writing final 3D video prompts.

## Website Conversion Rule
If generated video will be used as a scroll-driven website asset, plan framing, subject stability, pacing, and negative space for downstream frame extraction and canvas-based web storytelling in `video-to-website-skill.md`.

## Stability Rule
Maintain stable:
- logos
- faces
- hands
- products
- text overlays

Do not introduce distortion for style.

## Required Output
1. Brief interpretation
2. Video objective and audience/platform
3. Creative direction
4. Scene-by-scene structure
5. Camera and motion plan
6. Lighting and mood
7. Final prompt
8. Negative prompt
9. Export recommendations
10. Remaining risks
