## In-app guide: "How to use TaxiFlow" (built 2026-10-28)

What people see after signing in, so training can point at it:

- **When it opens:** automatically the first time a person signs in to a business (once per person per business, remembered in that browser/phone). It does not open on later sign-ins. A new phone or a cleared browser shows it once more.
- **How to reopen:** the **Help** button next to Sign out, at any time.
- **How to close:** Skip for now, the X, Escape, tapping outside, or any "Open ..." button. All of them count as "seen".
- **What it says, by role** (only screens the person can open are mentioned):
  - **Driver:** check your vehicle (choose the active vehicle if you drive more than one) -> add trips (Add daily earnings, Add extra trip) -> add costs you paid -> send your day with Checking and hand in the cash shown -> report vehicle problems in Fleet & Operations. Warns when no vehicle is linked and that Checking needs a saved trip first.
  - **Owner / Admin / Manager:** Overview -> capture the day in Money -> review, verify and bank the cash (states the person's own cash steps: count, verify, bank) -> record expenses -> fleet -> link drivers to vehicles -> reports -> Settings. Warns when Money is switched off for the account, and in a business workspace that a second person must verify every count. An Owner who also drives is told to use "Capture as: My own driving".
  - **Demo viewer:** look around Overview; nothing is saved.
- **Training tip:** ask a new user to read the guide's "Do this first" line, then press the main button; it takes them to the right screen.
