# Offer and Pricing

## Offer Name
Name the offer or product package.

## What Is Included
List features, service items, deliverables, support, onboarding, and handover materials.

## What Is Excluded
State what is not included to prevent scope creep.

## Pricing Hypothesis
List possible pricing options.

## Payment Structure
Examples: deposit + balance, monthly subscription, annual plan, once-off fee, setup + retainer.

## Upgrade Path
Describe how the customer can move to a higher package later.

## Support Model
Define what support is included and what is billable.
