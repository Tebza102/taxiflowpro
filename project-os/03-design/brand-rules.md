# Brand Rules

Updated 2026-10-10 (Task 19). The logo artwork is **approved**. A UI colour palette is **proposed, not approved**: the platform colours have not been changed.

## Approved originals (never edit; regenerate derived files from these)

| File | Content | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `assets/brand/source/taxiflow-logo-full-approved.webp` | Full TaxiFlow Pro logo (TF mark, wordmark, tagline, three bars), without the minibus | 1448 × 1086, off-white ground (RGB 253) |
| `assets/brand/source/taxiflow-symbol-approved.png` | TF symbol, for favicon and app icons | 301 × 155, off-white ground |

Derived files are generated by `node scripts/brand/generate-brand-assets.mjs` (ffmpeg, no new dependency). Dimensions are recorded in `assets/brand/brand-assets.json`. The generator:
- crops only the near-white margins;
- scales uniformly (never stretched or redrawn, and no SVG tracing);
- maps the off-white ground and near-white margin pixels (every channel ≥ 250) to pure white, so icons sit on solid white. Artwork colours are untouched.

**Source resolution:** the symbol image's artwork is only 289 px wide, too small for the 512 px icons without upscaling (upscaling would add no detail). The same mark appears in the approved full logo at 870–873 px wide. It was compared with the symbol image: aspect 2.219 vs 2.206, and a mean channel difference of about 2% after resampling. All icons are therefore cut from the full logo's mark, at higher resolution; the symbol image remains the approved reference.

**Rendered sizes:**
- Sign-in and picker logo: width `min(17rem, 72vw)`, about 272×158 px on desktop and 267×155 px at 375 px wide.
- App header logo: 4.5rem high, about 124×72 px. The wordmark is readable there; the small tagline is not legible at that size (an inherent limit of the full logo when small).

- The logo keeps its white ground. It always sits on light surfaces (the app has no dark theme), and drop shadows were removed because they would outline the white box.

**Favicon legibility:**
- 16 px reads as a dark T with a yellow and navy F block, but the swoosh detail is lost (the mark is 7 px tall).

**Removed (old brand):** `public/icon.svg`, `public/pwa-192.png`, `public/pwa-512.png`, `public/taxiflow-favicon.png`, `public/taxiflow-logo.png`.

**Caching:** the service worker cache is now `taxiflow-pro-v8`, and its app shell lists the new icon and logo paths. Activation deletes the v7 cache, so installed apps pick up the new branding. New paths under `/brand/` also bypass any cached old files.

## Logo rules
- Use only the generated files; never stretch, recolour, crop into the artwork, or redraw.
- Keep it on white or very light surfaces.
- For any future dark surface, use a white plate behind it. Don't add a shadow to the opaque logo.
- The minimum size for the full logo is about 120 px wide (the wordmark stays legible; the tagline needs about 260 px). Below that, use the symbol.

## Design Do Not Do
- Don't replace status colours (pending, counted, verified, banked, warnings, errors) with brand colours.
- No yellow or orange text on white.
